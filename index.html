05 · THE DESK PETS

PERSONNEL FILES OF A
VERY SMALL MACHINE.

Twenty-two specials, each a beloved computer moment given a body and a personnel file. Six of them, declassified:

01

BSOD JR.

"have you tried turning me off and on?"

Falls over :( then gets right back up. Files a crash report about itself each time. Sometimes takes the whole desk down with it — then apologizes to every single machine.

02

ARTIFACT

"do not re-encode me"

A compression artifact that gained sentience. Gives lossy hugs. Sheds corrupted macroblocks wherever it walks and calls them "texture".

03

Y2K BUG

"the year is 19100. probably fine"

The apocalypse that RSVP'd and never showed — still dressed for it. Counts down hourly. Once in a blue moon, the fireworks actually arrive.

04

PENGUIN CORE

"btw i use arch"

Monolithic, open-source, will explain itself unprompted. Traps colleagues in dependency hell; a Ctrl+C from a friend is the only rescue.

05

NOT A ROBOT

"select all squares with friends"

Passes every CAPTCHA first try. Suspiciously good at crosswalks. Occasionally runs a tiny 3×3 exam on a passerby — the verdict is always green.

06

CACHE GHOST

"you cleared me. i came back."

The cache you emptied, back from the beyond. Walks with a 1.2-second echo of itself. Refuses to be garbage-collected.

07 · THE ARTIST

YONGSHAN YU.

Canada-based pixel artist and software engineer. By day she leads AI and learning-platform engineering as a full-stack systems lead; by night she draws very small creatures, one pixel at a time.

Every Doodlebud begins as a hand-authored text template on an 11-pixel grid, rendered by a deterministic pixel engine she built herself. The engine is not a shortcut: each pixel on screen was placed there on purpose. She believes small art should still be careful art — and that the best characters are the ones you want to keep on your desk.
